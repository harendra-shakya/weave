## Positioning: The Curated Drop

**Statement:** Small, hand-picked sticker drops from real artists — the opposite of the marketplace flood.

**Why it's true:** A tight, defended catalog. Each drop is chosen, not dumped. Every design credits its maker. Every pack shows exactly what's inside, at true size, with the finish named.

**What it rejects:**
- Endless near-identical search results ("10,000 designs")
- Algorithmic sameness and dropshipped filler
- Mystery quality and misleading mockups
- Confusing, unparseable variant menus

**Supporting principle (product):** *What you see ships.* Honest previews at actual scale; size, finish, and pack contents stated before purchase. This principle drives the product-detail page and is the visual expression of the whole position.

## Name: Marginalia

- **Canonical form:** `Marginalia`
- **Pronunciation:** "mar-jin-AY-lee-uh"
- **Meaning:** The notes, doodles, and marks people leave in the margins of a book — exactly what the persona does to their planner. The name IS the ritual.
- **Stretch:** Extends naturally to washi tape, notebooks, and stationery without renaming.
- **Wordmark potential:** Margin-mark / handwritten-doodle motif; the "i" dotted like a hand-drawn mark; ink-on-paper feel.

## Narrative: The Frustration Story

**Premise:** You want a few beautiful sticker packs for your planner. Instead you get ten thousand near-identical results, no idea of the quality, and a variant menu you can't parse. The marketplace is broken. Marginalia is the curated fix.

**Hero (homepage):**
> # A few sticker packs worth the page.
> Not ten thousand you'll scroll past. Hand-picked drops from real artists — shown at true size, so you know what you're getting before it ships.

**About-page opening:**
> Search "planner stickers" and you'll drown — thousands of near-identical sheets, no idea what'll actually show up in the mail. We got tired of the gamble. So we curate small drops from real artists, show you exactly what's in each pack at true size, and tell you the finish before you buy.

**The hero (who the audience identifies with):** The planner hobbyist who wants their notebook to be a daily ritual and is tired of gambling on quality.

**Proof points (must stay true in the product):**
- Every design credits its **artist**.
- Every pack shows **contents at true scale** and states the **finish/material**.
- The catalog stays **small and curated** — drops, not an infinite grid.

## Mood: Stationery Studio

A well-lit stationery shop. Soft paper tones, curated calm, everything shown at true scale on the page — the opposite of a neon marketplace grid.

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Base** | Warm off-white / paper |
| **Accent** | **Sage + blush** — muted green + soft pink, used for accents/CTAs/tags; NOT neon |
| **Text** | Warm near-black ink |
| **Saturation** | Muted, restrained |
| **Type — headers** | Warm serif or light hand-lettering |
| **Type — UI** | Friendly humanist sans |
| **Imagery** | Flat-lay photography on dot-grid paper; stickers at **actual size**; a hand placing a sticker; visible paper texture |
| **Reference feel** | Papier, Present & Correct, Archer & Olive, Baum-kuchen |

**What this rejects:** Neon marketplace grids, glossy 3D mockups, dark mode, hype/drop-culture aesthetics.

---

## Voice quick-reference

- **Calm and curated, not hypey.** "A few worth the page," not "MEGA 1000-STICKER BUNDLE!!!"
- **Honest and specific.** Name the size, the finish, the count. No mystery.
- **The artist is credited; the buyer is a maker.** Copy respects both.
- **Never:** "bundle," "mega pack," "thousands of designs," "dropship," neon-sale language.
